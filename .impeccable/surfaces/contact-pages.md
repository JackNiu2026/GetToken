Use only the two cropped QR regions from supplied images. Do not display avatars, card backgrounds or invent contact expertise. Keep the QR in a white quiet zone, preserve native source pixels and verify scanner equality after responsive rendering. One h1, one QR and native links work without JavaScript. Current public navigation labels are reflected only within the new pages; existing site navigation is untouched. Keep names and QR assets paired by the manifest.

Desktop: left introduction and right QR panel; below 700px stack and keep actions close to the QR. Header links scroll in their own region on narrow screens.
